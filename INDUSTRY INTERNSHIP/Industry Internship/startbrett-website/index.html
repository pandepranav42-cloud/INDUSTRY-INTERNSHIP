/About

A Pune manufacturer built on quality and fair prices

Established in 2017 in Pune, Startbrett Private Limited is a manufacturer of control panels, industrial sensors and instrumentation — and a provider of NABL-traceable calibration, instrument installation and industrial automation.

Our story

Quality products, delivered on time, at fair prices

We build pump, motor, electric and custom control panels, and supply the sensors and instruments that go with them — RTDs, temperature and flow sensors, electromagnetic flow meters and more. Our products are valued for their quality, finish and affordable pricing.

Alongside manufacturing, we install and commission field instruments and provide NABL-traceable calibration, both in our lab and on your site. Our automation work earned a 1st-rank award at a national-level project competition held by Mitsubishi Electric.
